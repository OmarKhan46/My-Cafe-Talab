Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

Welcome to Cafe Talab. We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and website collect, use, and safeguard your details.

When you place an order or interact with our services via app, website, or direct channels, we may collect:

  • Contact Details: Name, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Order Details: Items purchased, order preferences, and delivery notes.

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Processing, preparing, and delivering your food orders.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Information is only shared with trusted service providers (such as delivery personnel) solely for order fulfillment.

4. Security

We implement standard security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

You may request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of your personal details stored with us by contacting us directly.
