Official Privacy Policy & Data Handling

Last Updated: 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Cafe Talab ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and related ordering services collect, process, use, and protect your data.

2. Information We Collect

To process your food orders and ensure accurate delivery, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Mobile phone number (used for OTP sign-in, account identification, and order verification), your name, and delivery address.
  • Location Information: Foreground location/GPS data (only when permitted by you) to help auto-detect your delivery address and route orders efficiently.
  • Order & Transaction Records: Selected food items, delivery preferences, order totals, and chosen payment method (UPI / Cash on Delivery).
  • Device & Crash Diagnostics: App performance metrics, operating system version, and crash logs collected via Firebase to improve app stability.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your identity via phone number verification.
  • To prepare, fulfill, and deliver your food orders.
  • To send order status updates and delivery communication.
  • To troubleshoot app crashes and optimize customer experience.
Our Policy: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services & Payment Security

We work with trusted third-party providers to operate our app:

  • Firebase (Google): Used for secure OTP authentication, database storage, and Crashlytics stability reporting.
  • UPI & Payment Apps: Payments are processed securely through installed UPI applications or official gateways. Cafe Talab does not collect or store your bank passwords, UPI PINs, or card details.

5. Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play developer policies, Cafe Talab provides clear paths for users to delete their account and associated data:

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. In-App Deletion: Open the Cafe Talab app, navigate to Profile / Settings, and tap Delete Account to permanently remove your profile.
  2. Email Request: Send an email from your registered address to cafetalab2023@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered 10-digit mobile number.
  3. WhatsApp Support: Message us at +91 9322653045 requesting account deletion.

What gets deleted:

  • Your phone number, profile name, saved addresses, and authentication records are permanently erased from our active database within 7 business days of verification.

What may be retained:

  • Anonymized financial order records may be retained for up to 90 days solely to comply with accounting, tax, and legal requirements, after which they are permanently expunged.
